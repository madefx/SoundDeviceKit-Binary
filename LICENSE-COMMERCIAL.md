## What a commercial license covers

A commercial license is a separate written agreement with MadeFX. Typically it grants the right to use SoundDeviceKit (the Swift package or the binary `SoundDeviceKit.xcframework`) in the licensee's commercial apps, and sets out the price, the number of apps, the term, update/support access and any redistribution rights. Nothing in this file is itself that agreement; until one is signed, only the [noncommercial terms](LICENSE) apply.
